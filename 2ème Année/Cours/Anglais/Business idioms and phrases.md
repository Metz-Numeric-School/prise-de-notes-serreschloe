## Notes OpenAI

OpenAI is an American company that specializes in artificial intelligence (AI), which means creating computer programs that can think and learn like humans. Its goal is to build safe and powerful AI tools that can help people in everyday life, education, business, and creativity. 

One of the most well-known products from OpenAI is ChatGPT, a chatbot that can have conversations, answer questions, write texts, solve problems, and much more. OpenAI has also developed other advanced tools, like DALL·E (which generates images from text), Whisper (which turns spoken words into written text), and Codex (which helps write computer code). 

These tools use huge amounts of information to “learn” how to understand and respond like a human would. OpenAI’s mission is not to replace people, but to support them—making work easier, faster, and more creative. With OpenAI’s technology, anyone can access the power of artificial intelligence to learn new skills, automate tasks, and explore their imagination.
